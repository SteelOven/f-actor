F-Actor repeat fine-tune: what is trained, and the approaches

A Llama-3.2-1B-sized backbone (16 layers, hidden 2048) that listens and speaks on 8 audio streams plus a text stream. The fine-tune teaches a repeat-the-word behaviour. This page shows which tensors move for each approach and which signal moves them; it carries no results. Sources: hf_models/f_actor/config.json, finetune_repeat/local_train.py, rows.py, make_dataset*.py, safetensors shapes.

3 · The training signal: one dialogue, two rows

One dialogue gives two rows, one per system perspective (rows.py): audio heads 0–3 are the system speaker, 4–7 the other speaker; the text stream, narrative, “starts the dialogue” flag and speaker embedding are the system speaker's. In training mode the loss covers every non-pad label of all 8 audio heads and the text stream; it is averaged per conversation and the audio and text parts are added. The orange words are the idea: what was heard has to come out again.

4 · Data approaches

Clips are TTS, tidied before assembly (lead cut to 0.1 s, word gaps ≤ 0.2 s, 40 ms fade-out, end tail 0.1 s). Validation uses words that are not in training, plus a few real rows as a sanity check.

5 · One layer, matrices drawn to scale

Areas are to scale (PyTorch [out, in]: rows = out, columns = in). Orange strips are LoRA A (r×in) and pink strips B (out×r); their thickness is exaggerated about 6×, real rank 16 would be under 1 px at this zoom.
